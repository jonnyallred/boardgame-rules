# Nemesis: Lockdown

## Overview

Nemesis: Lockdown is a standalone entry in the Nemesis series, played independently of the original Nemesis (though the two can be combined — see Special Rules). You and your fellow prisoners/workers must survive in a BSL-4 research facility on Mars that has been infested by hostile alien creatures. The rulebook calls the default creatures **Night Stalkers**, but uses "Intruder" as the generic/legacy term for the alien species and for shared components (Intruder bag, Intruder board, Intruder Attack cards, etc.) — treat "Intruder" and "Night Stalker" as interchangeable unless you're using the optional variant that swaps in the original Nemesis creatures (see Special Rules).

To win, you must complete one of the two Objectives dealt to you at the start of the game **and** survive until the rescue party arrives. The game may have multiple winners, but it is **not** a cooperative game: each player has their own Objective, and other players' success is irrelevant to your own victory. Obstacles include swarms of Night Stalkers, a rapidly depleting Power supply, other players' conflicting secret agendas (Objectives and Contingencies), and plain bad luck.

The game is played over a series of rounds, each split into a Player Phase (players act) and an Event Phase (Night Stalkers attack and move, the facility situation develops), and ends when any end-of-game condition is met.

## Setup

### Board setup (steps 1–14)

1. Place the board, basic side up (red arrow icon in the upper left corner).
2. Place these Room tiles on their marked slots: Isolation Room (side without a Room Action face up), Repository, Backup Power Supply.
3. Shuffle the remaining Room tiles "II" unseen and randomly place one face down on each "II"-marked slot; return the rest to the box unseen. (There are more tiles than slots — players never know exactly which Rooms are in the Facility.)
4. Do the same for Room tiles "I" on the "I"-marked slots.
5. Shuffle the Underground Exploration tokens unseen and place one face down on each unrevealed Room tile; return the rest to the box.
6. Place the Elevator, Main Gate, and Rover tokens on the board as shown.
7. Shuffle the 4 Power tokens and place randomly: 3 in the Section slots, 1 on the Elevator token. Tokens numbered 1–2 start **blue** side up (powered); tokens numbered 3–4 start **red** side up (unpowered).
8. Shuffle the 7 CSS tokens unseen and place one face down on each Time Track slot marked for a CSS token; return the remaining token to the box.
9. Place the Night Stalkers Laboratory board next to the main board with **5 Egg tokens** in the Nest slot and **3 random Night Stalker Weakness cards** (reveal the leftmost, leave the other two face down). Return the rest to the box. *(Unlike the original Nemesis, the first Weakness card is revealed from the start — though it can't be used until a Character researches an Object or is playing a Nemesis Survivor.)*
10. Fill the Intruder bag: 1 Blank, 4 Larvae, 1 Creeper, 1 Queen, 3 Adults, **plus 1 additional Adult per player.** Place the remaining Intruder tokens and the Carcass tokens next to the board.
11. Shuffle and place face down next to the board: the 3 colored Item decks, Night Stalker Event, Night Stalker Attack, Contamination, and Serious Wound decks. Shuffle the Computer Action deck, place it in its board slot, and reveal its top card (if "Lockdown" is revealed, reveal the next card instead and shuffle "Lockdown" back in). Place the Crafted Item deck next to the 3 Item decks, and the Scanner next to the Contamination deck.
12. Place the remaining markers/tokens/dice next to the board: Fire, Malfunction, Noise, and Ammo/Injury markers; Status markers; Door tokens; Character Corpse tokens; 2 Combat dice, 2 Noise dice, 2 Advantage dice; the First Player token.
13. Place the Alert Procedure, Autodestruction, and Venting tokens next to the board (used during play).
14. Place the Time token, inactive (red) side up, on the 15th (green) space of the Time Track.

### Crew setup (steps 15–21)

15. Deal 1 Help, 1 Contingency Reference, and 1 Night Stalker Reference card to each player at random (use the cards numbered 1 to the player count). The Help card's number is the **Player Number**, used for Character choice order and some Objectives. Help cards also set Player-Phase pass order.
16. Each player takes the plastic Inventory holder matching their Help card number.
17. Remove from both Objective decks (Corporate and Personal) any card showing a player count higher than the number playing. Shuffle each deck separately and deal each player **1 card from each deck**, kept hidden. *(You get your Objectives before the Character draft on purpose — pick a Character suited to your Objective.)* When the first Intruder is encountered, each player will choose 1 of these 2 and discard the other (see Special Rules).
18. Shuffle the Contingency tokens and deal 1 to each player (**2 each in a 2-player game**), kept secret. Place 1 Contingency token face down on the Active Contingency slot — this is the Active Contingency, secret until the end of the game. Place the remaining Contingency tokens in the Neutral Pool next to the Alert Room.
19. Character draft: shuffle the Character Draft cards. Player 1 draws 2, reveals them, keeps 1, and shuffles the other back into the deck; then Player 2, Player 3, etc. You may only control the Character whose Draft card you kept. Return unused Draft cards to the box.
20. Each player takes: (A) the Character board they drafted; (B) their Character miniature, placed in the **Isolation Room** (Nemesis Survivors — Survivor, Lab Rat, and any characters from Nemesis/Nemesis: Aftermath) or the **Repository** (Facility Workers — everyone else); (C) their 10-card Action deck, shuffled, face down at the left of their board; (D) their Starting Item card (if any) face up in a Hand slot, loaded with Ammo markers equal to that Weapon's capacity (some Characters, e.g. Lab Rat and Survivor, start with no Item); (E) their Quest Item, horizontal (inactive) side up, next to their board; (F) an empty spot beside their board for the Action discard pile; (G) their Knowledge token, inactive side up, on the Laboratory board — on **slot 1** of the Knowledge track for a Facility Worker, or **slot 3** for a Nemesis Survivor. Return unused Character boards and components to the box.
21. Player 1 takes the First Player token.

### Discard piles

Leave an empty discard space next to the Item, Night Stalker Event, Night Stalker Attack, and Serious Wound decks. Each Character board has a marked Action discard spot; Contamination cards go to the player's Action discard pile until removed from the game. All cards are discarded face up; markers return to their matching pool.

## Turn Structure

The game is played over consecutive rounds, each split into two phases:

### I: Player Phase

1. **Draw Action cards** — every player draws from their Action deck up to a hand of **5 cards**. If your deck is ever empty when you must draw, first shuffle your discard pile into a new Action deck, then draw.
2. **First Player token** — after everyone draws, the current holder passes the token to the player on their left (skip this on the game's first round).
3. **Player turns** — starting with the First Player and going clockwise, each player takes a turn of **2 Actions**, and turns continue in order until every player has passed, then proceed to the Event Phase.
   - If you can't or don't want to take an Action, you must pass; taking only 1 of your 2 Actions also forces a pass as your second Action.
   - Once you pass, you cannot perform any further Actions for the rest of the Phase (flip your Help card to its Pass side). Passing counts as taking an Action.
   - When you pass, you may discard any number of cards from your hand.
   - A Character who ends their turn in a Room with a Fire marker suffers 1 Light Wound — only once per round, even if other Characters still have turns to take afterward.

### II: Event Phase

4. **Launch CSS** — if a CSS token is under the Time token, reveal it and resolve which CSS Pods launch this round.
5. **Time Track** — move the Time token 1 slot right, then: (A) check whether the Autodestruction sequence or Alert Procedure must be resolved; (B) check Power Thresholds for a Power reduction.
6. **Noise removal** — in Sections with Power, remove all Noise markers from Corridors not connected to a Room containing a Character. (Never remove markers from the Technical Corridors space this way.)
7. **Intruder Attacks** — every Intruder sharing a Room with a Character attacks.
8. **Intruder Fire Damage** — every Intruder in a Room with a Fire marker suffers 1 Injury.
9. **Resolve 1 Event card**: (A) *Intruder movement* — every Intruder showing the card's symbol that is NOT already with a Character moves through the Corridor matching the card's number (two numbers = move first through the first-numbered Corridor, then the second; a move into a Room with a Character stops there; a move that would go through a Technical Corridor instead removes that Intruder from the board and returns its token to the bag; a move blocked by a Closed Door destroys the Door instead and the Intruder does not move); (B) *Event effect* — resolve the card's printed effect (some effects apply only in unpowered Sections, marked with a Darkness layout, in addition to the normal effect). Discard the card afterward unless told otherwise; if the deck ever empties, reshuffle its discard pile into a new deck.
10. **Intruder bag development** — draw 1 token from the Intruder bag:
    - **Larva** — remove it from the bag; add 1 Adult token to the bag.
    - **Creeper** — remove it from the bag; add 1 Breeder token to the bag.
    - **Adult** — return the token to the bag; all players roll for Noise in turn order (skip any player whose Character is in Combat).
    - **Breeder** — same as Adult: return the token, all players roll for Noise in order (Combat exempts you).
    - **Queen** — return the token to the bag. If any Character is in the Nest, place the Queen miniature there and resolve an Encounter; otherwise (or if the Nest is undiscovered) add 1 Egg token to the Laboratory board.
    - **Blank** — return the token to the bag; add 1 Adult token to the bag if one is available (otherwise nothing happens).
11. **End of round** — a new round begins with the Player Phase. The round only ends once bag development (including any Encounters/Surprise Attacks it triggers) is fully resolved.

## Actions

There are 4 groups of Actions: Basic Actions, Actions from Action cards, Actions from Item cards, and Actions from Room tiles.

**Action Cost:** the number on an Action's icon is how many Action cards you must discard from hand (face up) to pay for it. **Contamination cards are not Action cards and can never be discarded to pay a Cost**, even though they live in your Action deck. You must pay an Action's Cost even if the Action gets Interrupted by another player; if a One-Use-Only Item Action is cancelled by an Interruption, that Item is still discarded.

**Combat restriction symbols:** "In Combat only" Actions may only be used while an Intruder shares your Room (Eggs don't count as Intruders); "Out of Combat only" Actions may only be used otherwise. Unmarked Actions work in either case.

### Basic Actions (shared by every Character)

- **Movement** (regardless of Combat) — move to a neighboring Room, following all Movement rules below. In Combat, use Escape instead of a normal Movement.
- **Careful Movement** (out of Combat) — as Movement, but instead of a Noise roll after moving, place a Noise marker in a chosen Corridor connected to the destination Room. Not usable if every Corridor connected to that Room already holds a Noise marker, or while in Combat.
- **Pick Up Heavy Object** (regardless of Combat) — pick up 1 Heavy Object (Character Corpse, Intruder Carcass, or Egg) in your Room. (Heavy Items found via Search do not require this Action — it is only for Objects.)
- **Trade** (regardless of Combat) — start a trade with every Character in your Room; by mutual consent, any of them may reveal and exchange Item cards or Objects (Ammo cannot be exchanged). Only the initiator spends the Action, though several players may take part.
- **Craft Item** (regardless of Combat) — discard 2 Item cards showing the matching blue Component symbols to take the Crafted Item card sharing those symbols (in grey). You cannot craft an Item whose card isn't available, even with the right components.
- **Shoot** (in Combat) — attack an Intruder in your Room with a loaded Energy/Classic Weapon in Hand, discarding 1 Ammo from its card. (You may also Shoot uncarried Eggs outside of Combat — see Special Rules.)
- **Melee Attack** (in Combat) — attack an Intruder in your Room bare-handed or improvised. (Also usable on uncarried Eggs outside of Combat.)

### Actions from Action cards

Each Character has a partially unique 10-card Action deck. To use a card's Action, discard it face up from hand and pay its printed Cost (additional Action cards discarded beyond the card itself). A card with sections separated by "OR" lets you choose one.

### Actions from Item cards

Some Item cards grant an Action, described on the card itself.

### Room Actions

Described on the Room sheet. You must be in the matching Room, cannot use a Room Action while in Combat, and cannot use one in a Room with a Malfunction marker.

### Computer Actions

The Computer Action deck starts with 1 card revealed. Any Character in a Powered Section and in a Room with a Computer symbol may perform one of the 3 listed actions on the revealed card, then place it at the bottom of the deck and reveal the next card. Whenever a "Lockdown" card is revealed, resolve it immediately. A Malfunction marker in a Computer Room disables the Computer (as if there were no Computer symbol).

### Movement, Rooms, and Corridors

- Corridors connect Rooms; neither Characters nor Intruders can ever stop in a Corridor. Effects of moving through a Corridor resolve AFTER entering the Room.
- Two Rooms are neighboring only if directly connected by a Corridor; a Closed Door blocks that connection for Characters' Movement.
- Special Rooms (Alert Room, Elevator Rooms, Emergency Staircases) are treated as normal, already-explored Rooms, but have no Item Counter (can't be Searched) and are printed on the board.
- The Facility is divided into 3 Sections (S-01, S-02, S-03), each with its own color, Corridors, and Power source; the Elevator Power token counts as a separate 4th Section for Power-related effects. Emergency Staircases belong to no Section (always a different Section for Section-based effects) and are always in Darkness (treated as black Rooms with no Action); they may still hold a Malfunction marker, which does nothing there except reduce the shared Malfunction pool (emptying that pool destroys the Facility — see Scoring).
- **After entering a Room:** (1) if it was unexplored, flip the tile face up and resolve its Exploration token; (2) if the Room was empty (no Characters, no Intruders) before you arrived, perform a Noise roll — skip the roll if someone was already there.
- To leave a Room containing an Intruder you must use Escape (see Special Rules), not a normal Movement.

### Exploration tokens

There are two kinds — **Underground** (used on the basic board) and **Surface** (alternative board side only). Each token's front shows (1) the number of Items available in that Room, and (2) a special effect. Resolve in order:

1. **Set the Item count** — rotate the Room tile until the token's number faces the Item Counter symbol next to the slot (skip this for the Nest Room).
2. **Resolve the effect:**
   - **Silence** — nothing happens; no Noise roll for this Movement (even if triggered by Careful Movement, which still places its Noise marker). If you have a Slime marker, resolve Danger instead.
   - **Danger** — no Noise roll for this Movement. If an Intruder is in a neighboring Room and not in Combat, move it (and any other eligible Intruders) into this Room, destroying a Closed Door instead if that's in the way. If none are eligible, place a Noise marker in every connected Corridor lacking one (including Technical Corridors with an Entrance here).
   - **Slime** — place a Slime Status marker on your board.
   - **Fire** — place a Fire marker here.
   - **Malfunction** — place a Malfunction marker here.
   - **Door** — place a Door token in the Corridor you entered through.

Remove the token from the game after resolving it (each is used once, on first entry).

### Noise roll

Roll a Noise die (d10, or the d12 Mars Surface die on that board's surface areas) and resolve:

- **1–4:** place a Noise marker in the connected Corridor sharing that number (including a Technical Corridors Entrance, if present). A Corridor never holds more than 1 Noise marker — if told to place a second, resolve an Encounter instead.
- **Danger:** move any Intruder from a neighboring Room (not in Combat) into this Room — move all eligible Intruders (destroying a Closed Door in the way instead of moving through it). If none are eligible, place a Noise marker in every connected Corridor lacking one.
- **Silence:** nothing happens (unless you have a Slime marker, in which case resolve Danger instead).

Never roll for Noise when moving into a Room already containing a Character or Intruder — but note the risk: a shared Room can still trigger an Encounter via other effects, and grouping up is not always safe.

### Technical Corridors

Technical Corridors Entrances (some Rooms) and the single Technical Corridors space are inaccessible to Characters (except via the Survivor's Vents Action card, the Cave Entrance Room Action, and the Cave Plans Item). Otherwise they act as normal Corridors. A Noise marker that would go to an Entrance is placed on the Technical Corridors space instead, where it counts as present at **every** Entrance on the board (still capped at 1 marker — a second placement triggers an Encounter). Door tokens are never placed here. An Intruder that moves into a Technical Corridors Entrance vanishes into the ducts: discard its Injury markers, return its token to the bag, and remove its miniature (any Noise marker on the Technical Corridors space stays).

## Scoring / Victory Conditions

### Winning

There is no scoring. A player wins if their Character meets both:

1. **Fulfills their Objective** (the single card kept after the First Encounter).
2. **Survives**, meaning one of: **(a)** locks into the Isolation Room while the Facility is not destroyed, **(b)** evacuates via a CSS Pod, or **(c)** reaches the Bunker.

Multiple winners are possible; other players succeeding or failing doesn't affect your own result.

### End of the game

The game ends when any of the following happens:

- **The Time token reaches the final "S" (red) slot** — Corporation forces purge the Facility. Every Character still in the Facility who is not in the Isolation Room dies. The Intruders survive this (fail any Objective requiring the Queen dead or the Nest destroyed).
- **The Time token shares a slot with the red-side Autodestruction token and must advance further, OR you must place a 13th Fire marker (pool of 12) or an 11th Malfunction marker (pool of 10)** — the Facility explodes: every Character dies, including those already locked in the Isolation Room, and every Intruder (including the Queen) dies too.
- **The last living Character still in the Facility (i.e., not already in the Isolation Room) dies, locks into the Isolation Room, flees to the Bunker, or uses a CSS Pod** — there's nothing left to resolve. If the Autodestruction sequence was active, move the Time token onto its slot and resolve that effect; otherwise move the Time token to the final red slot and resolve that effect. Likewise, if the Alert Procedure was active, move the Time token to its slot instead of (or in addition to, if both are active) the other end conditions — all applicable end effects resolve.

If the game ends with at least 1 Character having survived (Isolation Room, Bunker, or CSS Pod), run the Victory Check.

### Victory Check (in order)

1. **Contingency check** — reveal the Active Contingency token and apply its effect to every surviving Character. (See Special Rules for what Contingencies represent.)
2. **Contamination check** — each surviving Character: (A) use the Scanner on every Contamination card in their Action deck, discard pile, and hand — if at least one is "INFECTED" (or if a Larva is already on their board, skip straight to step B), (B) shuffle all their cards (Action + Contamination) into a new deck and draw the top 4; if at least 1 Contamination card (Infected or not) is among them, the Character dies, otherwise they survive.
3. **Objective check** — every Character still alive reveals their Objective card; they win only if it is fully satisfied and they passed the Contingency and Contamination checks.

A Character who escapes, locks in, or dies stops actively playing (spectates only); their fate is settled at this final check.

### Objective types (rulebook guidance)

- Whatever your Objective, your Character must still survive (Isolation Room, Bunker, or CSS Pod).
- *"Player X's Character cannot survive"* — that Character must simply not be alive/escaped at game end; you needn't personally kill them (or you may escape/hide and hope the Facility, Fire, or Malfunctions finish them off).
- *"Your Character is the only survivor"* — no other Character may survive.
- *"No Nemesis Survivor can Survive"* — Nemesis Survivors are the Survivor, the Lab Rat, and any Character from Nemesis or Nemesis: Aftermath.
- *"Sending the Signal"* — perform the Transmitter Control Room's Send a Signal Action (this Room is always on the board, one of the "I" Rooms).
- *"Discover a Weakness"* — research any Object (Intruder Egg, Character Corpse, or Intruder Carcass) at the Laboratory (always on the board, an "I" Room); it doesn't matter who did the research.
- *"Destroy the Nest"* — no Intruder Eggs remain in it (deal at least 1 damage to each and remove its token); the Nest is always on the board (an "I" Room).
- *"Finish the game with at least Knowledge X"* — your Character's Knowledge must be ≥ X at game end.
- *"Activate your Knowledge token"* — perform the Archive's Room Action (always on the board, an "I" Room).
- *"All Rooms in Sections X and Y must be explored"* — every Room in both named Sections (of the Facility's 3: S-01, S-02, S-03, from the top of the board down) must be explored by game end.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases

### Critical gameplay moments

Four moments trigger outside the normal round structure:

- **First Intruder Encounter** — the first time any Intruder miniature appears on the board, every player discards 1 of their 2 Objective cards face down (removed from the game, never seen by anyone), then the triggering Encounter resolves. From then on each player has only 1 (still-secret) Objective.
- **First dead Character** — the Main Gate opens (remove its token) the first time any Character dies.
- **Isolation Room opens** — once the Time token reaches a white ("8") slot on the Time Track, Characters may lock themselves in the Isolation Room (not before). Activating the Alert Procedure opens it early.
- **Autodestruction becomes unstoppable** — once the Autodestruction token flips to its red side, the sequence can no longer be stopped (see below).

### Leaving the Facility

- **Cargo Sending System (CSS):** enter a CSS Pod via its Room's CSS Action (you must be in the matching Room and the Time token must share a slot with a CSS token). Entering discards your hand and passes your turn; place your miniature in that CSS Pod space, where you're unaffected by other game effects until the Event Phase. CSS Pods launch automatically in the Event Phase's Launch CSS step, in the round marked by a CSS token: reveal the token and resolve the outcome matching its printed icon — the rulebook describes three possible outcomes: the Character in that Pod space Evacuates (removed from the game) while anyone who didn't board is returned to the CSS Room and suffers 1 Serious Wound; **or** every Character present in that Pod space Evacuates; **or** no one Evacuates and everyone in that Pod space is returned to the CSS Room and suffers 1 Serious Wound.
- **The Main Gate and the Bunker:** the Main Gate starts closed (no one can reach the Bunker) and opens permanently on the first Character death, via certain Items/Actions in the Exit Room, or when the Autodestruction token flips red. Opening Doors elsewhere never affects the Main Gate. To Escape to the Bunker, a Character must be in the Exit Room (Room 002 on the basic board) with the Main Gate open, no Intruder present, and either the Rover in the Facility or an Enviro-Suit in hand — then: with the Rover present, simply move it and your Character to the Bunker (only 1 Character may ride); with an Enviro-Suit (no Rover), perform a Noise roll — if any Intruder appears, the attempt fails, otherwise you reach the Bunker; without either, you cannot attempt this Action. The Rover starts next to the Exit Room and can be remotely recalled to (or sent away from) the Facility via a Computer Action.
- **Contingency:** the Corporation's post-incident response. The Active Contingency stays secret until the Victory Check, when it resolves against every survivor. Players can trade or lie about the contents of their own (non-Active) Contingency tokens (Contingency Reference cards help do this secretly), and various Room/Computer/Item Actions let you peek at another player's tokens or at tokens in the Neutral Pool, helping you deduce the Active Contingency by elimination. When a Character dies, their Contingency tokens return to the Neutral Pool. In-world flavor names some possible Corporate codes — Green (evacuate everyone safely), Yellow (eliminate personnel with too much sensitive information), Blue (eliminate everyone except those with proper paperwork filed in time), Gray (destroy any CSS containers in transit), and Red (a nuclear payload obliterates the Facility) — but each token's exact effect is printed on the token itself.
- **Alert Procedure** (Alert Room Action): divide the Time token's current slot number by 2, rounding down, and place the Alert Procedure token there. The game ends instantly if the Time token ever leaves that slot. This also opens the Isolation Room immediately, letting Characters lock in even before a white slot.

### Autodestruction sequence (Cooling System Room Action)

Starting it places the Autodestruction token 3 slots ahead of the Time token (yellow side up, leaving 2 empty slots between them); while yellow, it can still be stopped via the Power Generator's Stop Autodestruction Action (even if a Character has already locked into the Isolation Room). During the Event Phase of the round it marks, it advances another 3 slots and flips to red — at that point it can no longer be stopped, and the Main Gate opens automatically if it hasn't already. When the Time token would have to move onto or past a red Autodestruction token, the Facility is destroyed that Event Phase (see End of the Game). If the token would run off the end of the Time Track, leave it on the last slot; it resolves at the game-end check.

### Encounters

An Encounter is any Intruder appearing (via an Intruder-bag draw) in a Room with a Character present; some Event and Intruder Attack cards can also trigger one. An Intruder simply moving into an occupied Room is not an Encounter (it's just Combat). Resolve:

1. Discard all Noise markers from Corridors connected to the Room (including Technical Corridors, if applicable).
2. Draw 1 Intruder token — it shows a species symbol on one side and 1 or 2 numbers on the other (see Night Stalker tokens below).
3. Place the matching Intruder miniature in the Room.
4. Compare the token's number to the number of cards in the triggering player's hand (Action **and** Contamination cards both count). Fewer cards than the number → Surprise Attack; equal or more → nothing happens.

Set the drawn token aside (it can return to the bag later if that Intruder type hides in the Technical Corridors). **Blank token:** place a Noise marker in every Corridor connected to the Room, then return the Blank to the bag (adding 1 Adult token to the bag if the Blank was the bag's last token and an Adult is available); the Encounter ends with no miniature placed.

**Night Stalker tokens (Darkness):** in Lockdown, Intruder tokens show 2 numbers. Use the lower number if the Character is not in Darkness, or the higher number if they are, when checking for a Surprise Attack.

**Adult overflow:** if all 8 Adult miniatures are already on the board when another must be placed, all Adults not currently in Combat retreat (remove their miniatures, return their tokens to the bag if possible), then place an Adult miniature in the Encounter's Room.

**Intruders in unexplored Rooms:** if an Intruder's movement ends in an unexplored Room, do not remove it or reveal the Room/its Exploration token.

### Combat

Sharing a Room with an Intruder puts a Character in Combat. Characters act in their turn (Attack or Escape); Intruders act in the Event Phase (or via Surprise Attack / Escape).

**Shoot:** (1) choose a loaded Weapon and 1 target Intruder in your Room; (2) discard 1 Ammo; (3) roll the Combat die — **in a Powered Section, use the Advantage die instead.** Combat die results: a miss; a hit (1 Injury) only if the target is a Larva or Creeper (otherwise a miss); a hit (1 Injury) if the target is a Larva, Creeper, or Adult (otherwise a miss); 1 Injury regardless of type; 2 Injuries regardless of type. The Advantage die shares all Combat-die rules (including rerolls) but swaps in two upgraded results: discard an Action card to deal 1 Injury; or deal 1 Injury, and optionally discard an Action card to make it 2. The Advantage die can never be used for a Melee Attack. Some Weapons modify these results (see the Weapon's card).

**Melee Attack:** (1) draw 1 Contamination card into your Action discard pile; (2) choose 1 target Intruder in your Room; (3) roll the Combat die (never the Advantage die). Same results as Shooting, except: any miss also inflicts 1 Serious Wound on you, and the "2 Injuries" result becomes only 1 Injury.

**Intruder Injuries and death:** place Injury markers on the Intruder equal to the damage dealt, then check:
- **Larva or Egg:** dies to a single Injury — remove it.
- **Creeper or Adult:** draw 1 Intruder Attack card and read only its "blood" number; if that number is ≤ the Intruder's current Injury markers, it dies (remove the miniature, place a Carcass token in the Room); otherwise it survives.
- **Breeder or Queen:** draw 2 Intruder Attack cards, sum their blood numbers, and compare the same way.
- The blood number represents current endurance and is re-rolled after every successful Attack (not just once per turn).

**Intruder Retreat:** if any blood-check card shows the Retreat arrow, the Intruder flees instead: draw an Event card, move the Intruder through the Corridor number given in that card's Intruder Movement section, then discard the card. Any Injury markers move with the fleeing Intruder unless it retreats into a Technical Corridor, in which case they're discarded along with the miniature. The Queen and Breeders retreat if **either** of their 2 drawn cards shows Retreat.

**Character Escape:** to leave a Room with an Intruder, perform a Movement Action to a neighboring Room; before moving, resolve 1 Intruder Attack per Intruder present. If you survive, complete the move as normal (Noise roll / exploration as applicable). If you die, place a Character Corpse token in the Room you tried to leave.

**Intruder Attack** (triggered by a Surprise Attack, the Event Phase's Intruder Attack step, or a Character's Escape): (1) choose the target — the Character with the fewest cards in hand among those present (ties go to whoever holds the First Player token, or is next after them in turn order); a Surprise Attack always targets whoever triggered the Encounter, an Escape attack always targets the escaping Character; (2) draw 1 Intruder Attack card — if its symbol matches the attacker's type, resolve its effect (some effects apply only in Darkness, marked with a Darkness layout, in addition to the base effect); otherwise the attack misses. Ignore the card's blood number during an attack. Discard the card after use; reshuffle the discard pile if the deck runs out.

**Infest (Larva attack only):** no Attack card is drawn. Remove the Larva miniature from the board; if the targeted Character doesn't already have a Larva on their board, place one there. The target also gains 1 Contamination card. If a Character who already has a Larva is Infested/Infected again (see Contamination below), they die immediately and a Creeper is placed in the Room.

### Contamination cards

When a Character gains a Contamination card (from any source), draw it from the Contamination deck onto the top of their Action discard pile. It behaves like a card in hand (can be drawn, held, discarded when passing) but never provides an Action and can never pay an Action Cost. Whether a given Contamination card is "INFECTED" is hidden until Scanned: place the Scanner's red foil over the card's coded text — the word INFECTED may or may not appear (watch for similar-looking decoy words). Various Actions/Room Actions describe their own Scan procedure. If a Scan reveals INFECTED, place a Larva on your Character board (keep the card); if you already have a Larva there, your Character dies instead and a Creeper is placed in your Room. Removed Contamination cards go to the bottom of the Contamination deck.

### Character Wounds and death

- **Light Wounds:** tracked on the Character board; the 1st marker sits in the upper slot, a 2nd moves it to the lower slot; a 3rd discards the marker and inflicts a Serious Wound instead.
- **Serious Wounds:** draw a Serious Wound card, place it face up by your board — its effect applies from then on. Duplicate Serious Wound cards on the same Character are not cumulative (but each still must be individually cleared).
- **Death:** a Character already carrying 3 Serious Wounds dies instantly from any further Wound (Light or Serious). Remove their miniature, place a Character Corpse token in that Room, and drop all their Heavy Objects there (other Items are removed from the game).
- **Dressing/Healing:** Dress a Serious Wound by flipping its card face down (its effect stops applying, but it still counts toward the 3-Wound death threshold). Heal removes a Light Wound outright or discards a Dressed Serious Wound card. Various Items and the Emergency Room Action (Dress all Serious Wounds, OR Heal 1 Dressed Serious Wound, OR Heal all Light Wounds) provide this.

### Knowledge and Weaknesses

Characters gain Knowledge by: Analyzing an Object (Carcass, Corpse, or Egg) at the Laboratory or via the Analyze Toolset (+3 Knowledge, discard the Object; gained whether or not a Weakness is discovered); certain Computer Actions (+1); discovering the Nest (+1, one-time); the Archive's Examine the Archives Action (Activate your Knowledge token, +2 — only usable while your token is inactive, and it also lets you peek at any Contingency token except the Active one); and various specific Item/Action effects. To discover a Weakness card, research an Object at the Laboratory: place the Object in the first empty Intruder-board slot and reveal the Weakness card beneath it. Weaknesses only benefit Characters who've reached the Knowledge threshold printed on them (3, 5, or 8) — Lab Rat and Survivor start at Knowledge 3, so they can use the first Weakness immediately. Analyzing the same Object type multiple times still grants Knowledge each time, but Heavy Objects are consumed (discarded) once researched.

### Power and Darkness

A Section has Power when its Power token shows blue. Powered Sections: (1) require the Advantage die for Shooting; (2) allow Computer Actions in Computer-symbol Rooms; (3) are never considered Darkness; (4) have their Noise markers cleared each Event Phase. Sections without Power are in **Darkness** — Darkness-tagged Event/Attack card effects apply, and Encounters there use the higher Night Stalker token number. Restoring Power (Backup Power Supply Room, S-03): unless 3+ Power tokens are already active, flip the 2 lowest-numbered Power tokens blue and all others red; then freely rearrange all Power tokens among Sections (without flipping them); if the Time token is inactive (red), flip it active (blue). The simpler Power a Section Room Action just flips that Section's own Power token, independent of the Time token's state. **Power Thresholds** (printed on the Time Track) reduce Power whenever crossed: red Thresholds only trigger while the Time token is on its red/inactive side; blue Thresholds always trigger. When a Threshold activates, flip the highest-numbered active Power token to inactive (red).

### Items and Objects

- **Sources:** a starting Item (most Characters), a Quest Item (unlocks mid-game once its printed condition is met), Searching, and Crafting.
- **Inventory:** normal (non-Heavy) Items are kept face down in your card holder, hidden until used (then revealed to all).
- **Heavy Items/Objects (Hand symbol):** never hidden; occupy 1 of your 2 Hand slots (max 2 at once) — to pick up a 3rd, Drop one first. Heavy Objects are the Intruder Egg, Character Corpse, and Intruder Carcass tokens.
- **Drop:** free, any time on your turn, no Action needed. Dropped Objects stay in the Room; dropped Items are lost, returned to the bottom of their deck.
- **Searching:** Item colors are Red (military), Yellow (technical), Green (medical), all found via a Search Action card matching the searched Room's color (a white Room lets you pick any 1 of the 3 decks). Searching reduces that Room's Item Counter by 1; at 0 it can no longer be searched.
- **Crafting:** the 4th, Blue, Item deck holds Crafted Items, which are never drawn — only obtained via the Craft Item Basic Action (discard 2 Items whose blue Component symbols match the target Crafted card's 2 grey symbols). You can browse the Crafted deck at any time. Buildable Crafted Items include the Neutralizer, Taser, Flamethrower, Enviro-Suit, and Pistol (each needs its specific pair of Components) — a Crafted Item can't be built if its card isn't available, even holding the right Components.

### Attacking other players

Characters cannot directly Attack one another (their corporate/crew implants forbid it), but may still harm each other indirectly — trapping someone in a burning Room, starting Autodestruction with others aboard, or throwing a Thermite Charge into an occupied Room.

### Fire, Malfunction, Door, Slime, and Noise markers

- **Fire (12 in the pool):** a Character ending their turn in a burning Room suffers 1 Light Wound (once per round); every Event Phase, each Intruder there suffers 1 Injury; running out of pool markers when one must be placed destroys the Facility (see Scoring). Max 1 per Room; discards return to the pool; Room Actions and Searching still work despite Fire; some Events spread it.
- **Malfunction (10 in the pool):** disables that Room's Action (Searching still works); running out of pool markers when one must be placed destroys the Facility. Max 1 per Room; never placed in the Nest or the Contaminated Room; disables Computers in that Room; a Malfunction in the Isolation Room does not affect Characters already locked in.
- **Door tokens:** live only in Corridors (max 1 each) and never affect Noise. Open = no token (default). Closed = standing token — blocks Movement (Characters and Intruders alike; Intruders that try to pass instead destroy the Door, and multiple Intruders moving together from the same Room all stay put while destroying it together). Destroyed = lying token — movement allowed, can never be Closed again. If the Door pool runs out, move a non-Destroyed token from elsewhere on the board.
- **Slime marker:** while you have one, every "Silence" result (Noise roll or Exploration token) becomes "Danger" instead. Only 1 at a time (extra Slime does nothing); removed via the Clothes Item or a Decon Room Action.
- **Noise marker:** exists only to flag Corridors for the Noise-roll rule; a marker in a Corridor with a Closed Door still affects the whole Corridor, not just one side.

### Playing with Characters from the original Nemesis

If you own the original Nemesis, its Characters can be used in Lockdown. During the Character draft, a chosen Draft card lets you pick either the Lockdown or the base-game/Aftermath Character of the matching color (e.g., the red Nemesis Soldier can stand in for the red Lockdown Survivor) — the two same-colored Characters can never both be in the same game. All Nemesis-sourced Characters count as Nemesis Survivors (starting Knowledge 3, starting in the Isolation Room). The **Pilot** uses the Pilot Action deck and Quest Item card provided in the Lockdown box itself (no Starting Item). The other original Characters (Captain, Scientist, Scout, Soldier, Mechanic) use their own Action decks from the Nemesis box, but their Quest Item cards from the Lockdown box, and start without a Starting Item. (Aftermath Characters' integration is explained in that expansion's own Stretch Goal Rulebook, not covered here.)

### Using the original Intruders instead of Night Stalkers (variant)

You can swap in the Intruder species/components from the original Nemesis box instead of the default Night Stalkers: use the Intruders' Laboratory board (on the back of the Night Stalkers board) with 5 Egg tokens and 3 face-down Weakness cards (remove the *Vulnerability to Fire* and *Susceptibility to Phosphates* Weakness cards first), swap in Intruder tokens for Night Stalker tokens, and use the Intruders' Event and Attack decks. Unlike the Night Stalker default setup, the first Weakness card is **not** pre-revealed in this variant.

### Game modes

- **Solo mode:** draw 2 Solo/Coop Objectives instead of the normal Objective cards (choose 1 at the First Encounter, as usual); each Room's Item count is halved, rounding up (Exploration tokens showing 1–2 Items set the counter to 1, showing 3–4 sets it to 2); win by fulfilling your Objective and surviving. After the Active Contingency is placed, draw 2 tokens from the Neutral Pool instead of 1.
- **Fully Cooperative mode:** place 1 Contingency token in the Active slot and the rest in the Neutral Pool; reveal 1 Neutral Pool token immediately (the rest are revealed over the course of the game, placed alongside it) — Contingencies are public information in this mode. Draw 1 Solo/Coop Objective per player (also public) instead of individual Objectives; **every** drawn Objective must be fulfilled, and at least 1 Character must survive, to win as a group (multiple cards requiring the Signal need that many different Characters to send it). **Revive:** the Emergency Room's AutoDoc can revive a near-death Character — another Character carries their Corpse token to a working Emergency Room, and at the start of the next round that player places their miniature there, discards all Light Wounds, and Dresses all Serious Wounds (doesn't work if an Intruder or Malfunction marker is present).
- **Alternative board side (Mars Surface):** usable with any game mode, though generally harder. During setup, use the reverse board side and place Surface Exploration tokens on all Crossroads and Building Rooms; keep the Mars Surface deck nearby. **Crossroads** are a special Room type that can never hold a Fire or Malfunction marker, connected to each other (and to Exit Rooms) by numberless Paths; Characters and (via Danger-type effects) Intruders can move along Paths, but Noise markers are never placed on them. All Mars-surface Crossroads count as connected to every **Area** for Noise-roll purposes — a Noise roll made on a Crossroads places its marker on the Area sharing that number, exactly like a Technical Corridors space. **Areas** surround individual **Buildings** and act like a Technical Corridor tied only to their one Building (effects that reference Technical Corridors generally, like Vents or the Cave Entrance, do not apply to Areas). A **Building**'s Rooms work like normal Rooms except: they're reached from a Crossroads via a Path-connected Exit Room; you may travel between Buildings on foot or by Rover; being inside a Building (or holding an Enviro-Suit) lets you apply a Mars Surface card's milder alternate effect instead of its default effect; a Noise roll inside a Building that matches that Building's Area places the marker on the Area as if it were a Corridor. Noise rolls anywhere on the Mars Surface use the **d12 Mars Surface die**: results 1–4 and Silence resolve normally, Danger resolves normally (in a Building) or moves all eligible Mars-surface Intruders to the rolling Character's Crossroads (or, with none eligible, places a Noise marker in every markerless Area), and its unique symbol instead draws and resolves a Mars Surface card (upper effect for most Characters; lower/milder effect for a Character with an Enviro-Suit or inside a Building). Surface Exploration tokens can also directly grant a card draw from the Red/Yellow/Green Item decks; their Malfunction-effect entry does nothing when revealed on a Crossroads. Moving via the **Rover** (based at an Exit Room) between Exit Rooms skips the Noise roll entirely; it can also drive a Character in its own Exit Room straight to the Bunker to Escape. On foot, a Character needs an Enviro-Suit to Escape to the Bunker from a Path-connected Crossroads: perform a Noise roll there, succeeding only if no Intruder appears.

## Player Reference

### Turn sequence

- **I. Player Phase:** 1) draw up to 5 Action cards; 2) pass the First Player token left (skip turn 1); 3) turns of 2 Actions each, clockwise, until everyone has passed.
- **II. Event Phase:** 4) launch any due CSS Pods; 5) advance the Time token (check Autodestruction/Alert and Power Thresholds); 6) clear Noise in Powered Sections; 7) Intruders in Combat attack; 8) Intruders on Fire take 1 Injury; 9) resolve 1 Event card; 10) Intruder bag development.

### Action costs

- Basic Action: 1 Action card (Careful Movement: still 1, but you place a Noise marker of your choosing instead of rolling).
- Action card: the card itself + its printed Cost in additional cards.
- Room Action / Computer Action: as printed on the sheet/card (not usable in Combat or a Malfunctioning Room for Room Actions).
- Contamination cards can never pay any Cost.

### Combat quick reference

- **Shoot:** pick Weapon + target, discard 1 Ammo, roll Combat die (Advantage die if Powered).
- **Melee:** draw 1 Contamination card, pick target, roll Combat die only; a miss also costs you 1 Serious Wound.
- **Escape:** choose a neighboring Room; resolve 1 Intruder Attack per Intruder present first; survive → complete the move; die → Corpse stays in the Room you tried to leave.
- **Intruder Attack:** targets whoever has the fewest cards in hand; draw 1 Attack card — matching symbol = hits, otherwise misses.
- **Intruder kill check:** Larva/Egg die to 1 Injury; Creeper/Adult draw 1 Attack card (blood ≤ Injuries = dead); Breeder/Queen draw 2 and sum. A Retreat symbol on the check sends the Intruder fleeing instead.

### Room Actions (Basic Rooms "I" — all present every game)

- **Archive** — Examine the Archives (only while your Knowledge token is inactive): gain 2 Knowledge, flip it active, then peek at any non-Active Contingency token.
- **Cave Entrance** — Move through Technical Corridors: move to a chosen explored Room with a Technical Corridors Entrance, then draw and resolve an Attack card (treat it as an Attack from an Adult Intruder in Darkness).
- **Cooling System** — Initiate Autodestruction (advance the Autodestruction token, yellow side up).
- **Decon Room** — Perform Decontamination: scan your Contamination cards in hand, remove INFECTED ones and any Larva; also discard a Slime marker if you have one.
- **Emergency Room** — Dress all your Serious Wounds, OR Heal 1 Dressed Serious Wound, OR Heal all your Light Wounds.
- **Laboratory** — Analyze 1 Object present (Carcass, Corpse, or Egg) for +3 Knowledge and to discover the Weakness beneath it.
- **Cargo Sending System A** — Enter the CSS Pod A (only when the Time token shares a slot with a CSS token and that Pod space is empty).
- **Nest** — Take 1 Egg token from the Laboratory board, then roll for Noise. (No Searching here; a Malfunction marker can never be placed here.)
- **Power Generator** — Stop Autodestruction (discard a yellow-side token) OR Power a Section (flip that Section's Power token).
- **Transmitter Control Room** — Send a Signal (place a Status marker on your Signal space), OR Check an Objective (a Character with a Signal marker must show you their Objective).

### Additional Rooms "II" (6 of 9 appear each game)

- **Cargo Sending System B / C** — as Cargo Sending System A, but for CSS Pod B / C respectively.
- **CSS Control System** — Schedule CSS: peek at a chosen CSS token and shift it 1 slot on the Time Track (never onto the Time token's own slot or another CSS token's slot).
- **Defense Control Room** — Exterminate the Threat: reduce the Item Counter by 1 or place a Malfunction marker here, choosing a Malfunction-free Room with an Intruder — every Intruder there suffers 1 Injury and every Character there suffers 1 Serious Wound.
- **Guard Room** — Take Equipment: reduce the Item Counter by 1 to take the Taser or Enviro-Suit Crafted Item.
- **Contaminated Room** — Searching here always also gives you a Slime marker (a Malfunction marker can never be placed here).
- **Surgery** — Perform a Surgical Procedure: scan all your Contamination cards (deck, hand, discard), remove INFECTED ones and any Larva, then automatically pass and suffer 1 Light Wound, shuffling your whole hand back into your Action deck.
- **Testing Lab** — Take Neutralizer: with 4+ Knowledge, take the Neutralizer Crafted Item.
- **Vent Control Room** — Vent Out: pick a Room with a Technical Corridors Entrance whose connected Corridors hold no Destroyed Doors; Close those Doors, remove any Fire marker there, and place the Venting token. If any of those Doors opens (or is destroyed) before the Player Phase ends, remove the token; otherwise everything in that Room (Characters and Intruders) dies at the end of the Phase.

### Special Rooms (fixed board position, explored from the start, no Searching)

- **Alert Room** — Start the Alert Procedure (see Special Rules), OR Peek at Contingency: take 1 token from the Neutral Pool after peeking at it.
- **Backup Power Supply** (S-03) — Restore Power (see Special Rules: Power and Darkness).
- **Elevator Rooms S-01/S-02/S-03** — Call the Elevator (bring the Elevator token to your Section, requires Power and not being in Combat); Use the Elevator (move yourself, the Elevator token, and any willing Characters in your Room to another Section's Elevator Room — no Noise roll).
- **Emergency Staircases** — always Darkness, treated as black Rooms with no Action; not part of any Section.
- **Repository** — Craft Equipment: Characters here may perform the Craft Item Basic Action for 1 fewer discarded card (only one of the two matching Component symbols is needed).
- **Isolation Room** — passive: a Character here at the start of a new round draws 1 extra Action card (up to 6), unless a Malfunction marker is present. Lock Yourself In (only once the Isolation Room is open — see Critical Moments): roll for Noise; an appearing Intruder fails the attempt, otherwise remove your miniature — you're safely locked in until the End of the Game check. (Other Characters can still start Autodestruction after someone has locked in.)

### Key numbers

- Hand refills to 5 Action cards each round (6 if starting your round in the Isolation Room, Malfunction permitting).
- 2 Actions per turn; most Actions cost 1 (Room/Computer Actions cost what's printed on them).
- Intruder bag starts with 1 Blank, 4 Larvae, 1 Creeper, 1 Queen, 3 Adults, + 1 Adult per player.
- CSS Pods: 7 CSS tokens seed the Time Track (1 returned to the box unused).
- 5 Eggs start in the Nest; 3 face-down Weakness cards, unlocked at Knowledge 3 / 5 / 8.
- 12 Fire markers, 10 Malfunction markers in the shared pools — a 13th Fire or 11th Malfunction placement destroys the Facility (everyone dies, Intruders included).
- Isolation Room opens at Time-Track slot "8" (white) or immediately via the Alert Procedure.
- 3 Serious Wounds + any further Wound kills a Character; 3 Light Wounds convert into 1 Serious Wound.
- Victory Check order: Contingency → Contamination → Objectives.
- Max 2 Heavy Items/Objects in hand at once; only 1 Character may occupy a given CSS Pod space at a time.
